# Leonida Route Map

## Overview
A pocket road atlas for someone playing GTA VI with a checklist beside the console. The first spread pairs the trophy ledger with one open field guide. Use the physical highway atlas specified in the brief, not a branded game menu or a metric dashboard.

## Colors
Green carries navigation and selected states. Grey is the sun-bleached binder and asphalt; warm white is the printed sheet. Reserve coral for the Chapter 1 warning and deep red for warning text on paper.

## Typography
Use signage type for the masthead, exit signs, and tracker names. Use a conventional sans for descriptions and longer instructions. Progress uses tabular figures. Trophy names and instructions must hold up when the cover is reduced to 60 percent.

## Layout
Logbook on the left, guide on the right; on narrow screens the selected guide moves before the list. The route runs down one dashed centerline, with numbered exits and grouped checkpoints. Settings occupy their own view, not a modal.

## Components
Use native checks, selects, and a search field. Grade shields are drawn, not logos. A ruled ledger groups trophy rows; selected rows get a pale green wash. Sources are underlined links and never preload.

## Do's and Don'ts
Keep corners square and borders quiet. Do not use artwork, fonts from the network, ornamental stats, shadows, gradients, or motion. Make the danger explicit in words as well as color.
