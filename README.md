Leonida Route Map is an offline road atlas for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI trophies on PS5. Save your checklist, keep unearned story trophies covered, and follow four exits from the Chapter 1 checkpoint to platinum. Start the local guide with `node serve.ts` and open `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.

## Sources

The trophy wording and grades come from the bounty’s PlayStation list, mirrored by [Exophase](https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/) and [TrueTrophies](https://www.truetrophies.com/game/Grand-Theft-Auto-VI/trophies). The original field guides cite [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/) and [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-and-roadmap) beside the relevant entries. The Chapter 1 meditation is marked potentially missable while its trophy requirement remains unconfirmed.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
